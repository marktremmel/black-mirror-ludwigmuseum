# Black Mirror · your pocket guide

**Ludwig Museum · "Black Mirror. The Long Shadow of the Future"**

Open this on your phone: **LINK HERE**

## Before the visit (at home, on Wi-Fi)

1. Open the link. A short tour shows you around (you can replay it later from ⚙).
2. Tap ⚙ → pick a voice you like (each has a ▶ try button) → **Save for offline** (about 35 MB).
3. Optional: *Share → Add to Home Screen*, so it opens like an app.
4. Bring **headphones** and a charged phone.

## At the museum

- **Pick a path** (A to E) at the top. The numbered dots on the map are its stops, in walking order.
- **▶ Play path as a podcast:** you hear a short intro, then each work. The voice tells you where to walk next.
- Or **tap any dot or work** to see the photo and text, then **▶ Listen**. The text lights up as it's read. Tap any sentence to jump there.
- **Where am I?** Point your camera at a work and the app guesses which one it is. (If it can't, long-press the map where you're standing.)
- Words with a **dotted underline** explain themselves when you tap them.
- **♡ Save** the works that get to you. They show up first when you write your reflection.
- **Remix Lab:** photograph a work, add a filter, stickers and a caption. It's highly recommended for your hand-in!

Speed, **text size**, music under the voice and the voice itself are all under ⚙.

## After the visit: reflection (10 to 15 min)

Go to **My picks → Start reflection**. There are no right answers, only your impressions and opinions. English please.

1. **Your top 3** works, each with one sentence.
2. **Vibe check:** 2 or 3 moods and a hope slider.
3. **Pick one:** argue with a work, *or* connect works that talk to each other, *or* invent a 35th work.
4. **Six words** about the exhibition, *or* be an archaeologist in 2080.
5. **Your remix** (highly recommended).

Tap **Make my PDF**, then send it to your teacher by email or Classroom **by the next lesson**. Your hand-in code is printed on the PDF.

*Nothing you do in the app gets uploaded anywhere. It all stays on your phone until you send the PDF.*
